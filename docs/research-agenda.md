# Research agenda

## Question
**How sensitive are logistics route decisions to explicit cost/time/risk trade-offs under disruption?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Small changes in objective weights can cause discontinuous route changes in networks with competing paths.

### H2
Explicit risk penalties reduce exposure at measurable cost/time trade-offs.

### H3
Multi-objective explanation improves auditability versus a single opaque route score.

## Experiments
1. Sweep weight triples over fixed synthetic networks and map route regimes.
2. Randomly remove/penalize edges and measure route stability.
3. Compare weighted-sum routing with constrained formulations in future versions.

## Metrics
- objective value
- cost/time/risk totals
- route-switch frequency
- disruption robustness
- regret against alternate objectives
